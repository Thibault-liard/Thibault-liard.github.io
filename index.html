Research

Research interests

My work lies at the interface of mathematical analysis, control theory, numerical optimisation and applications to transportation systems.

01

Traffic flow modelling and control

Mathematical modelling, observation, optimisation and control of road traffic using conservation laws and coupled PDE–ODE systems.

Collective dynamics
