Research

Research interests

My work lies at the interface of mathematical analysis, control theory, numerical optimisation.

Traffic flow modelling and control

Mathematical modelling, observation, optimisation and control of road traffic using conservation laws and coupled PDE–ODE systems.

Collective dynamics

Mathematical modelling of collective behaviour, with a particular focus on ant foraging and the interaction between population dynamics and pheromone trails.
